# Thinking, Fast and Slow

> "Your potential is determined by the habits you build and the mindset you bring to each day."

## Overview

Thinking, Fast and Slow by Daniel Kahneman was originally documented in 2015. It explores key themes in psychology through practical insights and actionable guidance.

## Core Concepts

### 1. Foundational Principle
System 1 (fast thinking) is prone to predictable biases.

### 2. Practical Application
System 2 (slow thinking) is lazy and often defers to System 1.

### 3. Key Insight
Knowing when to trust your gut and when to deliberate is itself a skill.

## Key Lessons

- System 1 (fast thinking) is prone to predictable biases.
- System 2 (slow thinking) is lazy and often defers to System 1.
- Knowing when to trust your gut and when to deliberate is itself a skill.
