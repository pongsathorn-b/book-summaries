# 12 Rules for Life — Jordan Peterson

> "Compare yourself to who you were yesterday, not to who someone else is today."

## Overview

Clinical psychologist [[30 Knowledge/People/Jordan-Peterson|Jordan Peterson]] delivers 12 principles for navigating existence. Part neuroscience, part ancient wisdom, part personal accountability. Each rule is a battlefield manual for [[Personal Responsibility|Personal Responsibility]].

## The 12 Rules

### Rule 1: Stand Up Straight
- [[Posture|Posture]] affects [[Serotonin|Serotonin]] and [[Mood|Mood]]
- Act like someone with purpose — your brain starts to believe it

### Rule 2: Treat Yourself Like Someone You Are Responsible For Helping
- Extend the same care you'd give a dependent to yourself
- If you wouldn't say it to a friend, don't say it to yourself

### Rule 3: Make Friends With People Who Want the Best For You
- [[Social Circle|Social Circle]] shapes who you become
- [[Proximity is Destiny|Proximity is Destiny]]

### Rule 4: Compare Yourself to Who You Were Yesterday
- Not to someone else — that leads only to [[Envy|Envy]] or [[Hubris|Hubris]]
- 1% improvement compounds into massive change over a year

### Rule 5: Do Not Let Your Children Do Anything That Makes You Dislike Them
- Children need [[Limits|Limits]] — limits create [[Security|Security]]
- If your child is annoying, you're failing to set boundaries

### Rule 6: Set Your House in Perfect Order Before You Criticize the World
- Fix what you can fix first before declaring the world broken
- Do the dishes. Pay your debts. Speak truthfully.

### Rule 7: Pursue What Is Meaningful, Not What Is Expedient
- [[Meaning Over Comfort|Meaning Over Comfort]]
- Meaning comes from [[Responsibility|Responsibility]] and [[Sacrifice|Sacrifice]]

### Rule 8: Tell the Truth — Or, at Least, Don't Lie
- Every lie is a debt you owe to reality
- [[Honesty as Strategy|Honesty as Strategy]]

### Rule 9: Assume the Person You Are Listening to Know Something You Don't
- [[Listen to Understand|Listen to Understand]], not to reply
- The goal of conversation is [[Discovery|Discovery]]

### Rule 10: Be Precise in Your Speech
- Vague language creates vague reality
- [[Precision|Precision]] dissolves confusion

### Rule 11: Do Not Bother Children When They Are Skateboarding
- [[30 Knowledge/Concepts/risk-tolerance|Risk Tolerance]] creates [[Resilience|Resilience]]
- [[Overprotection|Overprotection]] creates fragility

### Rule 12: Pet a Cat When You Encounter One on the Street
- [[Small Moments of Beauty|Small Moments of Beauty]] are real and matter
- The world is worth it — find the evidence
