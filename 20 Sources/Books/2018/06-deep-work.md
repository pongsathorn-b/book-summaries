# Deep Work — Cal Newport

> "Clarity about what matters provides clarity about what does not."

## Overview

[[30 Knowledge/People/Cal-Newport|Cal Newport]] makes the case that [[20 Sources/Books/2018/06-deep-work.md|06-deep-work]] is the skill of the 21st century. The ability to focus without distraction on a cognitively demanding task is increasingly rare — and increasingly valuable.

## Deep Work vs Shallow Work

**[[20 Sources/Books/2018/06-deep-work.md|06-deep-work]]:** Distraction-free concentration that pushes cognitive abilities to their limit. Creates new value. Hard to replicate.

**[[30 Knowledge/Concepts/shallow-work|Shallow Work]]:** Non-cognitively demanding, logistical tasks performed while distracted. Low value. Easy to replicate.

## The Four Rules

### Rule 1: Work Deeply
Develop rituals and routines to minimize [[30 Knowledge/Concepts/willpower|Willpower]] needed for [[20 Sources/Books/2018/06-deep-work.md|06-deep-work]].

- **Depth philosophies:** Monastic, Bimodal, Rhythmic, Journalistic
- [[Ritual and Routine|Ritual and Routine]] as [[Willpower Conservation|Willpower Conservation]]

### Rule 2: Embrace Boredom
Don't take breaks from distraction — take breaks from focus.

- Put your smartphone in another room during deep work
- [[Attention Restoration Theory|Attention Restoration Theory]]: [[30 Knowledge/Concepts/focus-is-a-skill|Focus is a Skill]]

### Rule 3: Quit Social Media
Apply the [[Craftsman Approach|Craftsman Approach]]: identify what matters most, keep tools that directly support it.

### Rule 4: Drain the Shallows
Protect [[20 Sources/Books/2018/06-deep-work.md|06-deep-work]] by ruthlessly eliminating [[30 Knowledge/Concepts/shallow-work|Shallow Work]].

- Schedule every minute deliberately
- [[Ambitious Scheduling|Ambitious Scheduling]] — finish by 5:30 to force focus

## [[Attention Restoration Theory|Attention Restoration Theory]]

Willpower is finite. The goal is to build [[Automaticity|Automaticity]] so deep focus requires less [[30 Knowledge/Concepts/willpower|Willpower]].
